A21 — Technion — Role in the Israeli Military-Academic Complex

The Technion contributed to the development of the remote-controlled D9 bulldozer, widely used in the destruction of Palestinian homes in Gaza and the West Bank. It developed an acoustic crowd-control weapon known as "The Scream." The Technion maintains research agreements and partnerships with Elbit Systems and Rafael Advanced Defense Systems. As Elbit's president has summarised: "The Technion is woven into Elbit's DNA." The Technion president expressed unconditional support for the Israeli military in a letter of October 2023.

Sources: +972 Magazine; PSL student investigation, May 2024; Elbit Systems; Maya Wind, Towers of Ivory and Steel (Verso, 2024); Technion official statements.

ujfp.org — Stop Academic Complicity report
A22 — Hebrew University of Jerusalem

Some university buildings are constructed in East Jerusalem, in violation of international law. The university hosts the "Havatzalot" military training programme. During Operation Protective Edge (summer 2014), the university created a special bursary fund for students participating in the assault on Gaza. In March 2024, it suspended Professor Nadera Shalhoub-Kevorkian following her signing of a ceasefire petition.

Sources: Maya Wind, Towers of Ivory and Steel; PSL student investigation, May 2024; Mondoweiss; Middle East Eye.

A23 — Tel Aviv University

Tel Aviv University hosts the "Erez" bachelor programme, a dual academic-military curriculum. Ministry of Defence funding conditions include an obligation for the university to report to the army "any security problems in the field." Tel Aviv University is the institution that developed the Dahiya doctrine, recommending the use of overwhelming and disproportionate force, the destruction of civilian infrastructure, and the disregard of collateral damage in military operations.

Sources: Maya Wind, Towers of Ivory and Steel; PSL student investigation, May 2024; INSS.
